03 / Before you call

Start at the back door.

A few photos and rough dimensions can give Scott a clearer picture of what will fit your space.

  1. Show the layoutTake a wide photo from the house and another from the far end of the yard.
  2. Note slopes and accessInclude steps, narrow gates and spots where water collects after snowmelt or rain.
  3. Decide what staysPoint out trees, fences and surfaces you want to keep, plus what you’d most like to change.
