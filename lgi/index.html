03 / Design inspiration

Start with the room you have.

Warm wood, a simple tile field and dark hardware give this bathroom a clear palette. For your own project, start with photos of the existing room and decide what you’d like to keep.

Before you pick a tile

Get the details right early.

A few decisions about the existing room can make it easier to compare options and explain the job.

Can I replace just a few damaged tiles?

Include a close-up and a wider photo, and mention whether you have spare tiles. The condition underneath and whether the tile can be matched help determine what’s practical.

What should I decide before retiling a shower?

Note whether the shower base and fixtures are staying. Think about the niche, wall layout and where the tile ends at the doorway or ceiling.

What matters for a Calgary entryway?

Think about wet boots, grit and snowmelt. Ask about slip resistance, easy cleaning and how the tile will meet the adjoining floor.
