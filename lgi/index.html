Before you begin

A few good
questions.

Can I replace only the damaged tiles?

Spare tiles and photos help establish whether a match is possible. The surrounding tile and the condition underneath will affect the scope of the repair.

What should I plan for a shower renovation?

Think about whether the base and fixtures will stay, where you want a niche, and where the tile will finish. Photos of the existing shower help explain those decisions.

What about a Calgary entryway?

Wet boots, grit and snowmelt make cleaning and slip resistance useful things to discuss. Consider the transition between the new tile and the adjoining floor.
